## Mathematical Formulation of LLM-SR

### **1. Foundational Objects**

*   **Function Space**: A Hilbert space $H$.
*   **Manifold Universe**: $\mathbb{M}$, a set of finite-dimensional immersed submanifolds of $H$.
*   **Criterion Space**: A partially ordered set $(C, \preceq)$.

### **2. Optimization Criteria**

*   **Objective Functional**: $L: H \to \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$.
*   **Property Functional**: $J: \mathbb{M} \to C_J$.
*   **Value Function**: $V: \mathbb{M} \to \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0} \times C_J \subseteq C$
    $$ V(M) := \left( \inf_{f \in M} L(f), \; J(M) \right) $$

### **3. Search Dynamics**

*   **State Space**: $\mathcal{S} := \mathcal{P}(\mathbb{M} \times C)$.
*   **Proposal Generator**: $G: \mathcal{S} \to \mathcal{P}_{fin}(\mathbb{M})$, such that for any $\mathcal{E} \in \mathcal{S}$, $G(\mathcal{E}) \cap \pi_1(\mathcal{E}) = \emptyset$.
*   **Process**: A sequence $\{\mathcal{E}_k\}_{k=0}^\infty \subset \mathcal{S}$ defined by:
    1.  $\mathcal{E}_0 := \emptyset$.
    2.  $\mathcal{E}_{k+1} := \mathcal{E}_k \cup \{(M, V(M)) \mid M \in G(\mathcal{E}_k)\}$.
*   **Termination**: The process terminates at step $K$ if $G(\mathcal{E}_K) = \emptyset$.
*   **Final State**: $\mathcal{E}_{final} := \mathcal{E}_K$.

### **4. Solution**

*   **Set of Minimal Criteria**:
    $$ C^* := \{ c \in \pi_2(\mathcal{E}_{final}) \mid \nexists c' \in \pi_2(\mathcal{E}_{final}) \text{ s.t. } c' \prec c \} $$
*   **Solution Set**:
    $$ \mathbb{M}^* := \{ M \in \pi_1(\mathcal{E}_{final}) \mid V(M) \in C^* \} $$
